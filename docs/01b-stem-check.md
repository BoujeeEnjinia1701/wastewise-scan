# WasteWise Scan STEM check

Verdict: the working principle of the scanner (near-infrared reflectance at discrete bands, read by one InGaAs photodiode and classified on band ratios) sits within known science, and no row fails after four fixes made in this session under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 (fix rule; WSC-DEC-001 F1 to F4). The first run failed one detail: the open-air reading cannot hold the window crosstalk under 0.5 % of the signal (S12, 10 %). The independent review then refuted two "within" verdicts and one "plausible" one: with the dark gap taken from the real reading time, a backed clear item carried 0.73 % against 0.5 % (S5, S7); at the peak of a half-sine drop pulse the cell strap reached 59 MPa against about 50 MPa (S18); and option B's lamps sat 0.2 mm from PETG (S36). The fixes are a baffle tube through the glass with a separate detector window (F1), a scan of four averaged cycles (F2; 0.64 s), an 11 x 10 mm strap (F3) and aluminium lamp sleeves in option B (F4). The review also showed that many rows rest on assumed inputs (cap, rim and tube leaks, the ambient change rate, screen reflectance, crush distance, LED drift coefficients, the IEC 62471 limits as understood without the purchased text), so those rows are now **plausible** until measured. Of the 23 claims of option A, **12 are within known science and 11 are plausible**; of the 7 claims of option B, 4 are within and 3 plausible. S12, S17, S18, S19, S22 and S23 cannot be closed by calculation (they need a built head, a drop test, measured LED coefficients, users or a labelled item set at TRL 4), so `stem_check` is **open**. The kit's verdict label reads "to confirm at TRL 3"; for these rows confirmation is TRL 4 work. Method and rules: STANDARDS section 20. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, which runs the calculation note's script `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` so the two never disagree; results in `docs/04-calcs/stem_results.csv`.

### The failed details and their fixes

**S12, window crosstalk (fix F1).** When the check was first run, S12 read 10 % of the signal against the 0.5 % limit: LED light reflected by the item-side surface of the single 25 mm window reached the photodiode through the glass under the baffle at about the level of the item signal (WSC-CAL-001 C5), and the open-air reading removes only the fixed part of it (C7). The verdict was **Fails, fix proposed**. Amish had accepted on 2026-09-25 the fallback of a baffle extended through the glass with separate LED and detector windows (option (b) of WSC-DDR-003 D11), to be made if the offset proved unstable; the STEM check shows on paper that it is, so under the fix rule the fallback is now in the design, model, drawings, CAL, BOM, build plan and PRS (PR-017). Every ray that reaches the photodiode must now enter the detector window from the item side, so the glass path is closed by geometry. The first estimate of what is left (0.010 %) was scaled from the Fresnel path; the reviewer showed that was the wrong path, so C8 now estimates the light that hits the tube wall directly and leaks through it or round the gap (sealed with black RTV): about 0.05 % of the signal, which the open-air reading removes, and 0.003 % for a 5 % change between readings. The wall needs an opacity of 2 x 10⁻² or better; carbon-black filament should give far more, but FDM layer gaps can leak, so S12 is **plausible** until a coupon and a built head are measured at TRL 4. Options weighed:

| Option | Crosstalk residual | Cost and build | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keep the open-air reading only | About 10 % of signal with a 10 % window change (C7) | None | Fails the 0.5 % limit (not chosen) |
| Anti-reflection coated window | About 1 % (coating cuts the 4 % surface reflection to about 0.5 %, an assumed typical value) | Coated window, price to assess | Still over 0.5 % |
| Baffle through the glass, separate detector window, gap sealed (chosen) | Glass path closed; wall leak 0.003 % on assumed values (C8) | One 8.4 mm cored hole, a 5.8 mm disc and an RTV bead; line prices unchanged, small disc to assess | Plausible, to confirm at TRL 4 |

**S5 and S7, dark gap (fix F2).** The reviewer found the 7 ms gap between a band reading and its dark readings inconsistent with a 14.1 ms reading. Taking the gap as one reading, the backed clear item carried 0.73 % against 0.5 %. The scan now runs four cycles of 17 readings of two conversions each (8.1 ms) and averages them, treating ambient changes about 140 ms apart as independent (an assumption): 0.21 % on a backed clear item, prompt threshold 1.24, 0.64 s per scan. The same change raises the weakest-band signal-to-noise ratio to 643, which meets the 3-sigma criterion of 600 the reviewer asked for in S3.

**S18, cell strap (fix F3).** At the half-sine peak (1.57 times the mean deceleration) the 10 x 8 mm strap reached 59 MPa against about 50 MPa. It is now 11 x 10 mm with counterbored screw heads: 34 MPa, margin 1.45, still resting on the assumed 1 mm crush (S17).

**S36, option B lamp heat (fix F4).** The reviewer judged a bulb 0.2 mm from PETG a likely failure; option B's model now puts each lamp in an aluminium sleeve.

The kit 1.9 model also showed that the head block had no light path below the LEDs (each LED face sat on about 1.2 mm of printed block); a 4 mm exit channel from each LED face to the window is now modelled. That is a modelling correction, not a change of what the design does; it is listed with F1 in WSC-DEC-001.

## Fields not relevant

- **Biology, microbiology, toxicology and epidemiology:** the scanner makes no biological claim and contains no living or biological material. Contact with contaminated waste is a handling hazard covered by the safety notes in WSC-PRC-001 and the hazard analysis WSC-RSK-001 (gloves; never use the result to judge whether waste is safe).
- **Physics, fluid mechanics, acoustics and vibration, nuclear and ionising radiation:** no fluid flow, no acoustic function and no ionising source. Vibration enters only as the drop case (S17, S18).
- **Chemistry, inorganic and environmental and atmospheric:** no reaction is used; the only chemistry claims are the polymer bands (S1, S13, S20) and the cell (S10).

## Concept options

- **Option A, eight-band LED scanner (current design):** no failed row after fixes F1 to F3; 11 rows plausible. Kept as the repo's design; the concept selection is with Amish (WSC-OPT-001, within 10 %).
- **Option B, tunable-filter spectrometer:** no failed row. S30, S31, S32 and S35 within known science; S33 (sweep time), S34 (five resins from 1,550 to 1,850 nm alone) and S36 (lamp heat, after fix F4) plausible and open. Option B would need the same four-cycle scan for sunlight, which is not yet counted in its 0.74 s. Not dropped; scored in WSC-OPT-001.
